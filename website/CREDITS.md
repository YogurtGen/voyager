# Voyager website credits

The website, stylized spacecraft illustration, procedural blue atmosphere, and browser controller simulation are original Voyager project work, under the repository's MIT source license.

The spacecraft is an illustration of the site's space theme. It does not show live mission data or claim NASA affiliation.

- Voyager builds on [WaterConsole](https://github.com/YogurtGen/WaterConsole), by YogurtGen and contributors.
- The disc carousel reuses the exact pure geometry and spring functions already included in Voyager's `src/renderer/vendor/disc-motion-reference.ts`, from **3D CD Disc Carousel**, by **Kedhareswer Naidu** (MIT). [Component](https://21st.dev/@kedhareswer/components/disc-cascade-carousel). The required notice is bundled in [CAROUSEL-LICENSE.txt](assets/CAROUSEL-LICENSE.txt). Website input handling and presentation are adapted for this page.
- Art direction references: [Halide Topo Hero](https://21st.dev/@shivendra9795kumar/components/halide-topo-hero), by Shivendra Kumar, for layered perspective; [Animated Shader Hero](https://21st.dev/@ravikatiyar162/components/animated-shader-hero), by Ravi Katiyar, for procedural atmosphere. Their authoring source and original shader are not bundled. This site's shader and spacecraft are original implementations of Voyager's visual brief.
- **Inter** by the Inter Project Authors, under the [SIL Open Font License](assets/INTER-LICENSE.txt). The font files are self-hosted.
- Product screenshots show Voyager's clean public build using isolated demonstration data. Controller Lab is the only included game. The carousel's other discs illustrate adding your games and creating your controls.
- The Voyager application's visual theme is inspired by Voyager from **Reverse: 1999**. Its third-party art and audio credits remain in [MEDIA-CREDITS.md](../docs/MEDIA-CREDITS.md). This website does not bundle those recordings or character illustrations.

The site loads no scripts, fonts or images from 21st.dev. Release metadata is requested from GitHub's public API, with verified download links retained when that request is unavailable. The site uses local storage only to remember the motion preference.
